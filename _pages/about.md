I'm Arel Akaunu, an Associate Data Scientist with an MSc in Data Science from Liverpool John Moores University and a physics background from the University of Cambridge.

My work brings together statistical reasoning, programming, and clear communication. I enjoy investigating a question, understanding the data behind it, and being precise about what the analysis supports.

## What you'll find here

My [MSc portfolio](/portfolio/) includes accelerometer-based activity recognition, Reddit data collection and sentiment analysis, Bayesian sampling, statistical simulations, and research into squash ratings. The project pages include the original evidence and openly discuss limitations and reproducibility.

I also write about topics that interest me in my [articles](/year-archive/).
